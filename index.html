How We Work

From Quarry Block to
Your Container — 6 Steps

A transparent, quality-controlled supply chain from quarry to port.

01

Requirement Analysis

We review your stone type, dimensions, slab thickness, quantity, target finish, and delivery port. Custom quote generated within 24 hours.

02

Quarry Block Selection

Our inspectors visit partner quarries in Rajasthan to select premium defect-free raw blocks with optimal pattern consistency and zero micro-fissures.

03

Precision Processing

Multi-wire gangsaw cutting, epoxy resin reinforcement on the back mesh, and automated mirror polishing calibrated to 90+ gloss units.

04

100% Quality Inspection

Each slab verified for surface flatness, thickness tolerance (±1mm), shade grouping, corner squareness. HD photo & video reports shared before crating.

05

Seaworthy Packaging

ISPM-15 fumigated wooden bundles with plastic wrap, foam corner pads, and heavy steel strap bracing — designed to survive ocean container freight.

06

Global Export Dispatch

Seamless port loading at Mundra or Nhava Sheva with full Bill of Lading, Certificate of Origin, Phytosanitary, and container GPS tracking.
